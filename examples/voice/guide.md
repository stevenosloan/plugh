# Guides

### One goal per guide, named in the title

Title a guide with the reader's goal as a verb phrase: "Sync a second machine", not "Multi-machine support".

Why: readers arrive with a task in mind and match it against titles.
When unsure: if the title can't be a verb phrase, the guide is probably reference.

### Say what they need first

Open with one sentence on what the guide does, then what to have ready: tern's version, files and accounts.

Why: finding out at step 6 that you needed something at step 1 is the worst part of following a guide.

### Numbered steps, one action each

Each step is one thing to do, then what they should see when it worked.

Why: the reader is switching between the guide and a terminal, and needs to find their place again.
When unsure: if a step has "and then" in it, split it.
